# User Evaluation Plan & Consent-Free Feedback Template

**Status:** Prepared, not conducted. Obtain supervisor's guidance about participation/consent if required by the university.

## Suggested participants

Recruit at least 5 participants if feasible: a mix of students, general customers and one person familiar with menu administration. Report actual sample size; never inflate results. No medical records or diagnoses are necessary.

## Tasks to observe (one participant at a time)

1. Open the restaurant directory and find a vegetarian meal.
2. Inspect calories, protein and serving size for the selected dish.
3. Change the recommended calorie target to 500 kcal and focus on higher protein.
4. Explain why the first recommendation was ranked.
5. Compare two dishes in the comparison table.
6. Optional admin-only session: edit a menu item's price and verify its public listing.

For each task record: completion (yes / partial / no), approximate duration, confusion/error points and comments. Avoid recording sensitive medical information.

## Questionnaire (1 strongly disagree – 5 strongly agree)

- Q1: I can find a restaurant and browse its dishes easily.
- Q2: The nutrition measurements and their units are clear.
- Q3: The recommendation preference form is easy to use.
- Q4: I can understand why the application recommends particular meals.
- Q5: I understand that the demo data are illustrative rather than restaurant-verified.
- Q6: I can understand the limitations of allergy information.
- Q7: The app is legible and usable on my device.
- Q8: Overall, the prototype makes comparing menu options easier.

**Optional open feedback:** What was confusing? Which feature was most valuable? What should improve before public release?
